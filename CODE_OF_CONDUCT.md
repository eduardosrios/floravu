# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Floravu project spaces should remain professional, respectful, and focused on the work. Contributors and participants are expected to communicate clearly, assume good intent when reasonable, and keep discussions constructive.

Expected behavior includes:

- Using respectful language.
- Welcoming relevant feedback and questions.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, design, documentation, or process.
- Respecting different levels of experience and different areas of expertise.
- Avoiding harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory language, or repeated disruption.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or intentionally derailing project discussions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make professional collaboration unsafe or hostile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, block participants, or take other reasonable action when behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

To report a concern, contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate GitHub channel.
